Hospital-Grade Cleanliness

Our Daily 4-Stage Hygiene Cycle

A clean environment keeps sickness away. Here is our rigorous routine every single school day.

07:30 AM • Pre-Arrival

Floor & Toy Sanitization

Classrooms are mop-sanitized with child-safe disinfectant. All soft and plastic toys are wiped thoroughly.

08:30 AM • Check-in

Health & Temperature Screen

Every child is screened for fever or cough before stepping inside to maintain an infection-free classroom.

12:30 PM • Post-Lunch

Midday Reset & Handwashing

Children wash hands with guidance. Dining tables and feeding utensils are sterilized and sanitized immediately.

07:00 PM • Evening Close

Deep Sterilization & Steam Clean

Comprehensive wash of bathrooms, diaper disposal zones, bedding, and air purification reset for the next morning.
