Hero Section Anatomy

Hero Section: Why Elements Are Placed Here

Understanding the visual hierarchy and psychological triggers at the top of the webpage.

1. Top Ribbon: "Seawoods' Trusted School Since 2013"

Position: Top left, directly above main headline.

Why It's There: Eye-tracking studies show parents scan top-left first. Highlighting "12+ Years" immediately proves Hunny Bunny is an established, trusted institution—not a new risky setup.

2. Floating 4.8★ Rating Card

Position: Top right floating glassmorphism card.

Why It's There: Third-party social proof (39 JustDial reviews). Parents validate friend recommendations by checking star ratings before reading further.

3. Headline: "Where Every Child's Story Begins ✨"

Position: Center hero headline.

Why It's There: Connects emotionally. Parents aren't just buying "a seat in playgroup"; they are seeking a loving start to their child's life story.

4. "Book a Free Visit" CTA Button

Position: Primary orange button with arrow icon.

Why It's There: High contrast color. Framing it as "Free Visit" removes financial pressure and encourages immediate WhatsApp clicks.

Trust Architecture

Promise Strip & 4 Pillars Rationale

How we pre-emptively solve the top 5 parent objections before they even scroll down.

The Orange Promise Strip

Items: CCTV Monitored · Daily Sanitisation · Trained Educators · Soft-Floor · Restricted Entry.

Strategic Purpose: Sits directly below the hero image. Before a parent asks *"Is it safe? Is it clean?"*, this high-contrast bar answers all 5 safety questions instantly.

Color Psychology of the 4 Pillars

Safety Card (Sky Blue): Blue evokes security & professional reliability.

Hygiene Card (Green): Green signifies cleanliness, health, and fresh environments.

Learning Card (Orange): Orange triggers energy, creativity, and playfulness.

Motherly Care Card (Gold): Warm gold conveys patience and maternal affection.

❤️ Motherly Care Feature:

The "Motherly Care" pillar badge features a beating heart animation (`heartBeat`), visually reinforcing that teachers provide genuine affection, not just supervision.

Security Architecture

Safety Section: Bank-Grade Security Feel

Why safety is styled in deep navy blue with radar wave icon animations.

24/7 CCTV Monitoring

Every classroom & common area is covered. Footage reviewed regularly & accessible to parents on request.

Controlled Entry & Exit

Children handed over strictly to verified parents with ID. Zero shortcuts or unauthorized handovers.

First Aid & Emergency Trained

All staff trained in emergency response. Full medical kit stocked and checked monthly.

🛡️ Design Rationale (Navy Blue Background):

We switched from light pastel to a deep gradient blue (`#0D47A1` to `#1976D2`) for the Safety Section. Deep blue creates a feeling of hospital/security-grade protection, reassuring parents that their child's safety is non-negotiable.

Hygiene Standards

Hygiene Protocol: Cleanliness as Curriculum

Addressing mothers' micro-concerns regarding toddler immune safety.

Step-by-Step Daily Routine

🌅 Morning: Disinfectant floor mop & toy sanitisation before first child arrives.

🍱 Mealtime: Supervised hand-washing ritual before & after every snack.

🌇 Evening: Deep clean of restrooms, toy tubs & changing bins.

🤧 Zero-Tolerance Illness: Sick children stay home; transparent health reporting.

Floating Soap Bubble Animation

The Hygiene Protocol box features dynamic rising soap bubbles (`@keyframes bubbleRise`), visually communicating freshness, health, and cleanliness.

👟 Key Hygiene Policy Highlighted:
