Safety & Security

Your child's security
is not a feature — it's our foundation

Every corner of Hunny Bunny is designed with little ones in mind. Here's exactly what we do to keep your child safe, every single day.

📹

24/7 CCTV Monitoring

Every classroom and common area is under continuous surveillance. Footage is reviewed regularly and available to parents on request.

🚪

Controlled Entry & Exit

Children are only handed over to verified parents or authorised guardians. No exceptions. No shortcuts.

🧱

Child-Proofed Infrastructure

Foam-tiled floors, rounded furniture, covered sockets, safety gates, and no sharp edges anywhere children play or learn.

🚑

First Aid Trained Staff

All teachers are trained in basic first aid. A well-stocked first aid kit is always accessible. Emergency protocols are rehearsed regularly.

🚦

Real Safety Education

Local traffic police visit to teach road safety. Fire drill sessions. Personal safety awareness — because the safest child is an informed one.

👩‍⚕️

Illness Protocol

Sick children are kept home. All staff follow strict hygiene practices. We communicate transparently with parents about any health concerns in the school.

Hygiene Standards

Clean classrooms.
Healthy children.
Peaceful parents.

Children's immune systems are still developing. We treat cleanliness as seriously as curriculum — and we want you to know exactly what we do.

🌅
Morning Routine — Before Children Arrive
Full classroom sweep, floor mopping with disinfectant, all toys wiped with child-safe sanitiser, fresh mats laid out. The room is clean before the first child walks in.
🍱
Before & After Every Meal
All children wash hands with soap — supervised and assisted. Meal surfaces are wiped before and after. No shared utensils.
🌇
Evening Deep Clean
At end of day, all mats, toys, and surfaces receive a thorough second clean. Restrooms sanitised. Bins emptied and changed.
🤧
Illness Policy — Zero Tolerance
Any child showing signs of fever, cold, or infection is kept home until fully recovered. Staff follow the same protocol. We notify parents immediately if there's any health concern in school.
🧼 Our Daily Hygiene Protocol
🦠
Child-Safe Disinfectants Only
No harsh chemicals. All products are non-toxic, baby-safe certified
👟
Shoe-Free Classrooms
All shoes removed at entrance — children & adults both
🧸
Toys Sanitised Daily
Every shared toy cleaned with child-safe wipes each morning & evening
🚿
Hand-Washing as a Lesson
We teach children the right way to wash hands — it becomes a daily habit
💨
Ventilated Classrooms
Natural airflow maintained throughout school hours. No stale air.
🏥
Medical Protocol Ready
First aid kit stocked & checked monthly. Emergency contacts on file
