Frequently Asked Questions by Parents

Does my child need to be potty trained before joining Playgroup?
No, absolutely not! Our caring lady helpers assist with diaper changes and gentle potty training routines in a warm, patient, and shame-free manner.
What is your classroom staffing ratio and class size?
Each class has 2 certified Teachers and 1 dedicated Helper active at all times, with strictly not more than 20 children in a class. This personal attention helps every child grow with confidence.
Do you have direct tie-ups with high schools for primary admissions?
Yes! We have official high school admission tie-ups with Orchids The International School, Tilak Public School, and Ryan International School. Our alumni also consistently secure admissions into Don Bosco, Podar International, Delhi Public School (DPS), Apeejay School, and Presentation Convent.
Can parents inspect the CCTV footage?
Yes, 100%. We have full transparent CCTV coverage across all classrooms and common areas. Parents are welcome to view footage anytime upon request at our reception desk.
What happens if my child cries on the first day?
Separation tears are normal and completely natural. We offer a gentle staggered settling period where parents can accompany the child during initial days until they feel safe and familiar with their teacher.
What is Hunny Bunny's fee policy for families of different financial backgrounds?
At Hunny Bunny, we believe every single child deserves loving, high-quality early education. We do not have rigid, commercial fee barriers. Our school founder personally consults with every parent — including car/auto drivers and working-class families — to arrive at a comfortable, adjusting arrangement so that no child is ever turned away from learning.
