100% CCTV Coverage

High-definition day and night-vision cameras cover all activity spaces, entrance corridors, nap suites, and play zones. Zero blindspots exist inside our facility.

Parent Pickup Token Rule

No child leaves the premises without the authorized parent or guardian presenting their registered pickup token badge. Strangers or unverified visitors are strictly prohibited.

Police-Verified Staff

100% of our teaching faculty, caretakers, and security personnel have completed police background verification, residence verification, and pediatric health clearances.

First-Aid Certified Care

Every classroom has emergency first-aid kits and educators trained in pediatric basic life support, accidental bumps, fever management, and immediate doctor coordination.

Childproofed Interiors

Rounded furniture edges, finger-pinch safety guards on doors, soft impact foam mats in play zones, and elevated child-safe electric switches.

Fire Safety & Drills

Functional certified fire extinguishers, unobstructed wide exit points, and quarterly safety roleplay drills where children learn basic emergency safety.

Infection-Free Guarantee

Medical-Grade Hygiene Cycle

Preschoolers touch everything and explore with their hands. Our multi-stage daily disinfection protocol ensures illnesses don't spread between children.
